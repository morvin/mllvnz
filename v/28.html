80 themed puzzles, coloring pages and mazes. A relaxing culinary journey through Italy. Large print.

Descrizione

Taste the magic of Italian tradition through a relaxing journey of words!

In Italy, food is never just nourishment: it is history, culture, family, and love. Sitting at the table means sharing a piece of life. This large-print word search book is designed to take you on a mouth-watering culinary journey through the regions of the Italian peninsula while providing a relaxing, screen-free brain workout.

Inside this delicious puzzle book, you will discover:

  • 80 Themed Puzzles: Dive into four incredible courses covering traditional First Courses & Pasta, rich Meat & Fish dishes, beloved Pizzas & Street Food, and mouth-watering Desserts & Coffee.
  • Large Print Format: Easy-to-read grids and perfectly sized fonts, ideal for adults and seniors to completely avoid eye strain.
  • Bonus Activities: Unwind with beautifully illustrated food-themed coloring pages and engaging perfect mazes interspersed throughout the book.
  • Mindful Relaxation: A wonderful way to practice mindfulness, improve your focus, and reduce daily stress while dreaming of your next Italian meal.
  • Complete Solutions: Conveniently located at the back of the book, just in case you need a little help.

Make yourself comfortable, perhaps while the water is boiling in the pot or with a good steaming coffee next to you. Enjoy the search for each word as if you were savoring your favorite dish.

Get your copy today, have fun, and... Buon Appetito!
