Apri la scheda su Amazon

80 puzzles celebrating America's greatest wild places: from Yellowstone and Yosemite to Denali, the Everglades and beyond. Large print.

Descrizione

There is no country on Earth that protects its wild places the way America does.

In 1872, the United States did something no nation had ever done before: it set aside a piece of wilderness — Yellowstone — and declared it forever protected, forever wild, forever for the people. Today, 63 national parks guard the most extraordinary landscapes on the planet, from the coral reefs of Florida to the glaciers of Alaska, from the steaming calderas of Hawaii to the red-rock canyons of Utah.

This word search book is a tribute to all of them.

80 large-print puzzles take you on a coast-to-coast journey through America's crown jewels, organized in four chapters:

  • Icons of the American West — Yellowstone, Yosemite, Grand Canyon, Glacier, Rocky Mountain, Olympic, Crater Lake, Redwood, Mesa Verde and more.
  • Desert Skies and Canyon Depths — Arches, Zion, Bryce Canyon, Death Valley, Joshua Tree, Saguaro, Canyonlands, Carlsbad Caverns, Badlands.
  • The Wild East and South — Great Smoky Mountains, Acadia, Everglades, Mammoth Cave, Shenandoah, Congaree, Biscayne, New River Gorge.
  • Peaks, Glaciers and Wild Shores — Denali, Glacier Bay, Wrangell-St. Elias, Gates of the Arctic, Hawaii Volcanoes, Isle Royale, Katmai, Kenai Fjords.

Each puzzle is accompanied by a short, vivid description of the park — its geology, its wildlife, its history, its secrets. You will discover that Death Valley holds the world heat record of 134°F and still blooms with wildflowers after rain. That Crater Lake is the purest large body of water in North America. That the wolves of Yellowstone, reintroduced in 1995, reshaped the course of rivers simply by changing where the elk grazed.

This book is perfect for:

  • Adults and seniors who love large-print puzzles with real substance
  • Nature lovers, hikers, birders, and national park passport collectors
  • Anyone planning a road trip or bucket-list park adventure
  • A meaningful gift for people who love the outdoors and America's wild heritage

Whether you have stood on the rim of the Grand Canyon or only dreamed of it, every puzzle in this book is an open door to one of the greatest places on Earth.

Solutions included at the end of the volume.
