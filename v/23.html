Descrizione

Taste the magic of Italian tradition through 80 mouth-watering word search puzzles!

In Italy, food is never just nourishment: it is history, culture, family, and love. Sitting at the table means sharing a piece of life. With this book, we want to take you on a culinary journey through the regions of the peninsula, rediscovering the names of the dishes that mark the Italian lifestyle.

From fresh pasta dishes to grandma's famous desserts, discover a truly relaxing pastime to gently train your mind. Engaging in word searches, coloring, and navigating mazes is a wonderful way to reduce stress, improve your focus, and disconnect from daily screens. Treat yourself to a moment of pure analog mindfulness!

Inside this book you will find:

  • 80 Themed Puzzles: Divided into four delicious courses: First Courses & Pasta, Main Courses (Meat & Fish), Pizzas, Breads & Street Food, and Desserts, Coffee & Traditions.
  • Large Print Format: Clear, easy-to-read grids to prevent eye strain.
  • Bonus Content: Beautiful coloring pages and relaxing mazes hidden among the puzzles.
  • Solutions Included: Conveniently located at the back of the book.

Make yourself comfortable, perhaps while the water is boiling in the pot or with a good steaming coffee next to you. Enjoy the search for each word as if you were savoring your favorite dish. Have fun and... Buon Appetito!
