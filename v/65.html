Plan your trip to Ischia: the complete guide to Italy's green island in the Gulf of Naples.

Descrizione

Ischia isn't a destination you rush through. It's one you slow down for, one town at a time.

This Golden Mapas guide takes the independent traveler to discover Ischia, the largest island in the Gulf of Naples, through thermal parks, beaches, and fishing villages.

What you'll find in this guide:

  • Trip preparation and logistics: how to get there from Naples or Pozzuoli, getting around the six towns, documents and budget
  • Hot springs and wellness: a real, opinionated guide to the island's many thermal parks, so you can pick the right one for you
  • Themed itineraries: routes for wellness, the nature of Monte Epomeo, the less crowded beaches, and local food and wine
  • History and Myth: from the Cup of Nestor to the giant Typhon imprisoned beneath the island, the stories that shape its identity
  • The six towns: a dedicated profile for each municipality, to help you choose where to stay based on your own pace of travel

With themed itineraries, practical tip boxes, and a full index, this guide helps you choose wisely and experience the island at its most authentic pace.

Your journey to Italy's green island in the Gulf of Naples starts here.
