Apri la scheda su Amazon

80 totally tubular puzzles with bonus coloring pages and mazes. A nostalgic journey through 80s music, movies, fashion and arcade games.

Descrizione

Are you ready to travel back in time to the most awesome decade?

Big hair, neon colors, synth-pop music, and the dawn of the digital age. The 1980s were a time of bold expression, unforgettable entertainment, and groundbreaking technology. This word search book is a celebration of everything that made the decade so iconic, designed to offer you a moment of calm and gentle brain stimulation.

Whether you lived through the 80s or just love the retro vibe, put on your favorite mixtape, get comfortable, and let the nostalgia flow while unwinding with hours of screen-free fun.

Inside this totally tubular book, you'll find:

  • 80 Themed Puzzles: Journey through four immersive chapters dedicated to Music & Fashion Icons, Movies & TV Hits, Games & Technology, and Life & Culture.
  • Large Print for Ultimate Comfort: Featuring easy-to-read grids and clear fonts to provide a strain-free experience for adults and seniors.
  • Bonus Activities for Extra Fun: Discover beautifully illustrated coloring pages and engaging mazes thoughtfully placed throughout the book for even more relaxation.
  • The Ideal Gift for 80s Fans: A wonderful present for birthdays, holidays, or any occasion for anyone who loves retro pop culture.
  • Complete Solutions: All solutions are included at the back, so you can peek if a word is hiding a little too well.

From the birth of MTV to the golden age of arcade games, every word you find is a piece of a puzzle that defined a generation. It's the perfect way to de-stress and treat yourself to a moment of pure analog mindfulness.
