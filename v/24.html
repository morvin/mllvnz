80 themed puzzles to celebrate the awakening of nature. Large print mindful activity book with bonus coloring pages and mazes.

Descrizione

Celebrate the awakening of nature with 80 word search puzzles dedicated to the most colorful season of the year!

The days get longer, the air gets sweeter, and nature is tinged with a thousand colors. Spring is the ideal time to recharge your energy and dedicate time to yourself. This book is a tribute to rebirth, to be leafed through outdoors kissed by the first warm rays of the sun.

Among blooming flowers, outdoor walks, and sunny days, discover a truly relaxing pastime to gently train your mind. Engaging in word searches, coloring, and navigating mazes is a wonderful way to reduce stress, improve your focus, and disconnect from daily screens. Treat yourself to a moment of pure analog mindfulness!

Inside this book you will find:

  • 80 Themed Puzzles: Divided into four immersive chapters: The Awakening of Nature, Flowers and Gardens, Easter and Traditions, and Trips and Leisure.
  • Large Print Format: Clear, easy-to-read grids to prevent eye strain.
  • Bonus Content: Beautiful coloring pages and relaxing mazes hidden among the puzzles.
  • Solutions Included: Conveniently located at the back of the book.

Make yourself comfortable in the garden or on the balcony, perhaps with a cold drink. Take your time: searching for words in these pages will be like taking a regenerating walk in a flowery meadow.
